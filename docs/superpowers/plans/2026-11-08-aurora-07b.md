## Interfaces

AnimationController(actor) owns mixer/action state; Character.requestAction(name,{duration}) overrides movement until completed, Character.update retains old signature and character.state diagnostics. Character.cutscenePose supports cutscene ownership.
CutsceneManager({camera,actors,light,dialogue,effects,onLock,onFinish,onError}) implements play({id,shots}),update(dt),pause(),resume(),skip(),dispose(), active/paused. Shots are bounded declarative actions; all teardown restores owned state.
QuestDirector({getState,save,onReward,onChange,definitions}) implements start(id),emit(type,target,payload),choose(choice),current(id),snapshot(),static validate/fresh exported helpers. Adventure states live under progress.adventure.
Adventure mounts actual interactable targets via InteractionRegistry, reuses Story panels and Dialogue, drives CutsceneManager and Character actions. Main never teleports through a test API.
